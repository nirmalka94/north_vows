The Eye Behind The Lens

We believe in quiet, meaningful details.

Abin Founder & Lead Photographer

With 5 years of experience as a destination wedding photographer at Magicmotionmedia and Vows of Love, Abin brings a heartfelt, cinematic eye to every session. His work captures the quiet moments — a stolen glance, a tender touch — that tell the real story of your day.

North Vows Photography was founded on the philosophy of visual simplicity. We step back to let moments unfold naturally, focusing on light, space, and quiet connections that often go unnoticed in the rush of life.

We photograph weddings, families, portraits and professional headshots across Canada. Based in Kitchener, Ontario, we serve Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge and the surrounding region.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll get back to you shortly.

Which areas do you cover? Is there a travel fee?

We are based in Kitchener and travel within Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge at no extra cost. For other locations in Ontario and across Canada, we add a travel fee that we quote upfront. We only take bookings within Canada.

How soon will we receive our photos?

Delivery depends on the size of your event. We agree a delivery date with you when we plan your package, so you know exactly when to expect your gallery.

How do we book our date?

Send us your date through the form or WhatsApp. Once we confirm availability, a signed agreement and a retainer secure your date. The balance is due one week before the event.

How many photos will we get?

It depends on the package and the length of your session. We confirm it with you before you book, so there are no surprises. Every image is individually edited, never just filtered.

Do you offer videography?

Yes. Videography is available on its own or together with photography. Choose "Photography + Videography" in the contact form and we will send a combined quote.

Can we print our photos?

Yes. Every gallery comes with high-resolution downloads and a personal print release, so you can print anywhere you like.
